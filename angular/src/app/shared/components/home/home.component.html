Library Rules

  1. The library is open to bonafide students only.
  2. Students must wear their identity card inside the library.
  3. Enter after registering in the gate register and punching ID card.
  4. Silence must be maintained at all times.
  5. Eatables are not allowed.
  6. Books from reading hall must be returned before leaving.
  7. Library is not responsible for personal belongings.
  8. Misbehavior may lead to strict disciplinary action.
  9. Photography and video recording are prohibited.
  10. Mobile phones must be switched off.
  11. Keep belongings at the counter (except valuables).
  12. Identity and library cards are non-transferable.
  13. Smoking and tobacco use are strictly prohibited.

Address: Upskill Library, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India
